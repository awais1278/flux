Features

Everything you need to ship faster

Powerful tools designed to eliminate busywork and keep your team focused on what matters most.

Real-time Sync
Every change syncs instantly across all devices. No refresh, no delay, no confusion. Your team stays aligned, always.
Smart Analytics
AI-powered insights surface bottlenecks before they become blockers. Predict delivery timelines with 95% accuracy.
Enterprise Security
SOC 2 Type II, SSO, audit logs, and end-to-end encryption by default. Your data is safer than in a bank vault.

Connect Slack, GitHub, Figma, Jira, and every tool your team already uses. One platform, zero friction.
AI Assistant
Auto-generate summaries, draft status updates, and predict blockers. It's like having a project manager who never sleeps.
